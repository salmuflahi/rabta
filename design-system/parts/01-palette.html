Palette

Seven hues, and no eighth. Orange means exactly one thing — the live thing, or the primary action — so it can never be spent on decoration.

Petrol --petrol  #102526

The ground. Every surface starts here.

Orange --orange  #ff6b2c

Connected, active, primary. One meaning only.

Ivory --ivory  #f3f0e8

Text that carries the sentence.

Cool panel --cool-panel  #173239

A surface lifted off the ground.

Cool mid --cool-mid  #66858c

Dormant states, quiet marks. Not body text.

Cool soft --cool-soft  #a9bec2

Secondary text, borders, the sentence under the sentence.

Cool field --cool-field  #d9e3e3

The light end. Inverted panels and fields.

Measured against petrol: ivory 14.03:1, cool-field 12.21:1, cool-soft 8.24:1, orange 5.62:1, cool-mid 4.03:1. Petrol on orange is 5.62:1. Cool-mid clears 3:1, so it is legitimate for UI boundaries and large text but never for body copy.

There is no black. Both hairlines derive from ivory — rgba(243,240,232,0.08) and …0.14. A single rgba(0,0,0,…) rule was the one token whose base was not an approved hue; it was removed.
