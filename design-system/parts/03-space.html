Space & rhythm

A 4px base, eleven steps. With one typeface and almost no colour, the beat is most of what holds a page together — so section padding is a single value with no exceptions.

Section rhythm. --section-y: 128px on every top-level section, stepping to 96px under 900px and 72px under 600px. Three named variants exist for deliberate exceptions — --section-wide 160, --section-standard 128, --section-close 96.
