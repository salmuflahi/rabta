The return

One settling curve, one spring for a landed moment, four durations. Anything that comes back moves the way the leg of the mark does: out, then a curl back to rest.

--easecubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) for everything that settles.
The springstiffness 260, damping 18. The last stroke of the mark, a restore completing.
--dur-hover 120msA button lifting, the rule under a link drawing.
--dur-state 240msA row selecting, the focus switch, a beat changing in the pinned sequence.
--dur-reveal 480msA section arriving as it scrolls in.
--dur-ceremony 900msThe whole entrance of the restore sheet.
The mark, 1100msStem 420 from 0, bowl 560 from 180, leg 640 from 560, then the spring. The same numbers in website/js/brand.js and apps/desktop/src/lib/motion.ts.

Scroll is a timeline, not a trigger. Nothing fires once and disappears if the reader scrolls back. Under reduced motion everything collapses to a fade and the mark is simply there.
