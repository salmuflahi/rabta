Duration & easing

One curve for everything: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Durations are named by what they are for, so feedback cannot drift the way per-rule values already had once.

120ms — --durState changes that are not hovers. The default.
180ms — --hover-durEvery hover on the site, without exception. One number so the whole surface answers at the same speed.
140ms — --resolve-stepOne beat of the restore sequence. Rows are multiples of it, so the rhythm is a single number rather than four hand-picked delays.
420ms — --mark-durA chapter mark drawn once on entry. Slower than a hover, so it reads as an opening rather than a response.
520ms — --fold-durationThe receipt fold, after a 100ms delay.

Reduced motion is a real branch, not a switch-off. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the fold, the restore sequence and the counters resolve straight to their finished state — the information still arrives, it just does not travel.

Anything that plays on its own can be paused (WCAG 2.2.2), and a deliberate pause outranks scroll: once a visitor has paused a clip, scrolling back into view will not restart it.
