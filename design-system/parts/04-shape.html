The underside is orange, and it is the only place a large orange field is allowed — it is the inside of the paper, not decoration.

Geometry. The animated fold rotates about the hinge midpoint with rotate3d(1, 1, 0, …), not rotateY. rotateY mirrors horizontally rather than across the 45° hinge and leaves a visible notch where the two triangles should meet; the underside's clip-path is mirrored to match. There is a test pinning the hinge endpoints at every phase.
