The wordmark

Rabta is written, not monogrammed. The approved wordmark is one outlined path, rounded and lowercase, and it is the whole identity: the sidebar, the nav, the Dock icon, the favicon, the social card. The earlier R mark is retired and no longer ships anywhere.

One colourPaper on ink, ink on paper, or currentColor wherever it is inlined. The ember never touches the letters.
The tileInk wordmark at 80% of an ember squircle. Dock icon, favicon, avatar. Regenerated from wordmark.svg by scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs.
Clear spaceOne cap height on every side; never below 22px tall as a wordmark, 16px as a tile.
NeverOutline it, rotate it, stretch it, put it in a circle, add a shadow, or set the name in a typeface instead of the outlined path.
