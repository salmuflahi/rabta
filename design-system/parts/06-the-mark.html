The mark

An R whose leg is the Arabic letter ر from رابطة. Three strokes, drawn in the order the product happens: the stem, the bowl, the leg. The leg is the ember.

Two-toneStem and bowl in the text colour, leg in the ember. The default on ink and on paper; the nav, the sidebar, the hero.
The tileInk glyph at 64% of an ember squircle. Dock icon, favicon, avatar. Monochrome, because it has to survive 16px.
The lockupThe stem scaled to the cap height of Inter, then the letters a b t a as outlines, 0.11em after the leg. Clear space of one stem width; never below 22px tall.
NeverOutline it, rotate it, put it in a circle, add a shadow, or draw the leg in anything but the ember or the colour of the mark itself.
