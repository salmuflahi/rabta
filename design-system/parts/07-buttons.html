Buttons

Two, and a size. There is one primary action on a page, because orange means one thing — a second filled button would spend it.

.button--primaryOrange ground, petrol label. Sweeps rgba(243,240,232,0.22) — ivory over orange lightens the surface, so the label's contrast rises across the sweep instead of falling.
.button--quietBordered, not filled. Sweeps --cool-panel. For the second action in a row.
.button--small38px min-height, 13px. A size, not a third variant.

The quiet border is 0.55, not 0.32. An outlined control's border is its boundary. At 0.32 it measured 2.05:1 against petrol, under the 3:1 that SC 1.4.11 asks of a UI component; 0.55 gives 3.48:1.

Minimum height is 44px on the default size — a pointer target, not a text box.
