400–600, declared. The @font-face declares font-weight: 400 600 rather than the full variable range — 300 and 700 are outside the system, and declaring a wider range is what invites them back in.

Mono is the system stack. A downloaded mono face would fail CI's no-third-party-subresource guard, and the desktop app renders the system one anyway.
