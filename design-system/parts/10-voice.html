Voice

The system's rules for words, which are as much a part of it as the palette. Say what is true now; never what should be true.

Name the state, then the move “Rabta isn’t running. Open Rabta and this reconnects on its own.” Not “Connection error.”
Claim only what is measured “Reopened when you resume that task” — not “comes back exactly as you left it,” which shipped in the README and in site.webmanifest before anyone checked whether it was true.
A number beats an adjective “Tabs in view: 7” — and 7 means the seven pages that would actually be captured, not the ten tabs the window happens to hold.
No progress theatre Markup ships the offline case on purpose: a worker that never replies leaves an honest message rather than a spinner.
Sentence case, no exclamation marks Including in buttons. The product is calm.

Where this keeps being tested: the site said the browser connector was “in review” in three places while the listing was live. Copy about state goes stale exactly like an asset does, and nothing fails when it happens.
