Voice

Words are design material. Say what is true now; never what should be true. Plain verbs, sentence case, one register.

Name the state, then the move“Rabta isn't running. Open Rabta and this reconnects on its own.” Not “Connection error.”
Claim only what is measured“5.5 MB, macOS 11+, Apple Silicon only, no Intel build.” Every number on the site is the artifact's own, and a test pins it.
Partial means partialA restore reports per tool. If the demo shows a partial restore, the caption says so.
No dashes doing the work of sentencesThe brand's own copy carries no em-dash. Two sentences, or a colon.
Sentence case, no exclamation marksIncluding in buttons. The product is calm.

Where this keeps being tested: copy about state goes stale exactly like an asset does, and nothing fails when it happens. The tests of the site read the prose for the claims that matter.
