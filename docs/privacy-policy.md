# Rabta — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

Rabta is a **local-first** developer tool. The Rabta desktop app and its
connector extensions (VS Code / Cursor, Chrome) run entirely on your own
machine. **No data ever leaves your device**, and there is no Rabta server,
account, or cloud.

## What the Chrome extension accesses

- **Open tab URLs and titles** (`tabs` permission) — read only to snapshot which
  tabs belong to a task and to reopen them when you restore that task.
- **Local extension storage** (`storage`) — holds the pairing token that lets
  the extension talk to the Rabta desktop app.
- **Alarms** (`alarms`) — used to keep the local connection alive.

## Where that data goes

Only to the **Rabta desktop app running on the same computer**, over an
authenticated local connection (`127.0.0.1`) that **you explicitly approve**
the first time the extension connects. That's the only destination. The data is
never sent to us, to any third party, or to any remote server.

## What we do NOT do

- No remote servers, no cloud sync, no accounts.
- No analytics, tracking, telemetry, or advertising.
- No selling, sharing, or transferring of your data to anyone.
- Nothing is captured until you save a capsule; nothing is read while the
  extension is not connected.

## Data storage & control

Any workspace snapshot ("capsule") you save is stored locally by the Rabta app
on your machine, under your control. Uninstalling the extension or the app, or
deleting a capsule, removes the corresponding data. You can reveal the app's
data folder from **Settings → Privacy & data → Reveal in Finder**.
