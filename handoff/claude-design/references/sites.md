# Sites to study, and the one thing to take from each

Study the motion and the storytelling, not the layout. Nothing here is to be copied.

## Product story

- raylight.app: the reference for camera work. Measured moves, hard cuts, pushes that commit, a resting tilt of 36 to 48 degrees, nothing floats.
- family.co: the best scroll film on the web. One object carried through the chapters; the story told by transformation, not by sections.
- linear.app: restraint and truth. Product screens as the hero, type doing the selling, zero decoration.
- arc.net and diabrowser.com: a browser with a personality; playful without being childish; footage as the argument.
- resend.com: a dark developer brand with a micro-interaction in every component; a hero object that answers the cursor.
- raycast.com: a Mac developer tool that feels native. Dark, sharp, fast.
- superlist.com: motion everywhere without chaos; kinetic type; a cursor with moods.
- reflect.app: dark with light in it; glow used as a material. The Night studio direction lives here.
- granola.ai: a calm product story for people who are busy.
- zed.dev, warp.dev, cursor.com: how developer tools position without hype, and what this audience is used to.

## Instrument and material

- teenageengineering.com: the Instrument direction. Graphite, engraved, exact.
- daylightcomputer.com: paper and calm as a stance; light as the subject.
- nothing.tech: hardware brand discipline; dot-matrix type used with restraint.
- cosmos.so: editorial dark; images that breathe.

## Craft: cursors, transitions, smooth scroll, pinned chapters

- lusion.co, igloo.inc, unseen.co, exoape.com, basement.studio, studiofreight.com, obys.agency, locomotive.ca, activetheory.net: page transitions where an element travels, custom cursors with states, smooth scroll, pinned chapters, type that reacts to the pointer.
- darkroom.engineering (the makers of Lenis): the smooth-scroll standard.
- rive.app and spline.design: motion tools whose own sites move well. Watch how they hold 60 fps.

## Scroll-scrubbed film

- apple.com/airpods-pro: video scrubbed by the scroll; the product turns as you move; type arrives on beats.

## Fresh ideas

- awwwards.com/websites/animation: the last 90 days of winners, for cursor, transition and scroll mechanics. Take mechanics, not looks.
