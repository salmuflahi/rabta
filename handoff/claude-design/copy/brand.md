# An R that is also a ر.
Two alphabets, one glyph, three strokes. This page is the mark: where
it comes from, how it moves, the colours it lives in, and the files to
use it.
## Three strokes, in the order the product happens.
The stem: you start a task, and something comes down to rest. The
bowl: you capture, and the loop closes around what is open. The leg:
you leave, and you come back. That third stroke is the Arabic ر of
رابطة, and it is the one that gets the colour.

### The stem
You start. A task is a name for the thing you are doing, and something comes down to rest.
### The bowl
You capture. The loop closes around the files, the terminals, the tabs and the branch.
### The leg
You leave, and you come back. The stroke goes out and returns. This is the ر, and this is the brand.
## Rabta is رابطة: a bond, a link, the thing that ties things together.
The product is exactly that. Your tools each remember their own piece
of a task; Rabta is the bond between them. The name is pronounced
RAHB-tah, and it is written in the two alphabets the mark carries.
Rabta
Set in Inter, weight 560, the face of everything else on this site.
رابطة
The ر is the second letter, and it is the leg of the mark. Set in Reem Kufi, a geometric Kufi whose strokes share the mark's logic.
Three faces on this site, all under the SIL Open Font License and
each shipped beside its licence: Inter for everything, Reem Kufi for
the name only, Geist Mono for what is checkable: receipts,
terminals, code, hashes, paths.
## The mark, then the rest of the word.
The R's stem is scaled to Inter's cap height, so the mark and the
letters after it share a cap line. The wordmark ships as outlines and
never depends on a font being installed. Clear space around the
lockup is one stem-width; the smallest it should ever be set is 22px
tall, and the tile should never go below 16px.
Never outline the mark, rotate it, put it in a circle, add a shadow, or draw the leg in anything but the ember or the mark's own colour.
## Paper, ink, and the tile.
Two-tone is the default on paper and on ink: the stem and bowl in the
text colour, the leg in the ember. The tile is the mark in ink on an
ember squircle, for the Dock, the favicon and any avatar. Mono is for
the places two colours are not available.

## Paper, ink, one ember.
Both worlds are neutral with a cool cast, so a screenshot of the dark
app sits naturally on the light site. The ember is spent once per
view: on the primary action, or on the live thing, never both.

## Everything that comes back moves the way the leg does.
One settling curve for anything that comes to rest, and one spring
for the moment something lands. The mark draws itself in just over a
second, stem first, leg last. A window returns by rising into place;
a headline rises from behind its own baseline; a restore completes
with the ember. Under reduced motion, all of it collapses to a fade,
and the mark is simply there.
