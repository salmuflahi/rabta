Contact
# Talk to a human.
No support desk, no ticket queue. Rabta is built and maintained by one
person, so every channel below lands in the same place. What differs is
how quickly it gets read and what shape it needs to be in.
Sammy Almuflahi · maintainer
## Three places, and the difference between them.
Bugs & crashes
### Open an issue
Something broke, restored wrong, or refused when it shouldn't
have. The three things that make a report actionable are your
macOS version, which connectors were running, and what you
expected instead.
github.com/salmuflahi/rabta/issues → github.com/salmuflahi/rabta/issues
Questions & ideas
### Start a discussion
Workflow questions, connector requests, or how something works.
Public and searchable, so the next person finds the answer too.
The most useful requests describe the situation you were in, not
the feature you designed.
github.com/salmuflahi/rabta/discussions → github.com/salmuflahi/rabta/discussions
Everything else
### Email us
Press, licensing, partnerships, or anything that doesn't belong in
a public thread. Bugs and feature requests are still better as
issues: they stay visible to everyone else who hits the same
thing, and they don't get lost in a thread.
support@rabta.build → support@rabta.build
## Something security-sensitive.
Please don't open a public issue for it. Use GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting,
which reaches the maintainer without disclosing anything publicly, or
email support@rabta.build
directly. A fix ships before the details go public.
Rabta's whole claim is that nothing leaves your machine. If you find a
case where that is not true, that is the most valuable message this
project can receive, and it will be treated that way.
Response times are a person's, not a company's. Security reports come
first; everything else gets read in order. No autoresponders: replies
usually land within a couple of days.
## One canonical home.
The source, the releases and the issue tracker all live on
GitHub.
That is the canonical home; anything else is a mirror of what is
there.
If a restore did something surprising, the Activity view records what
each connector was asked to do and how it answered. That log is local
to your machine, nothing is sent anywhere, so pasting the relevant
part into an issue is usually the fastest route to a fix.
